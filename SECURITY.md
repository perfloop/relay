# Reporting a vulnerability

Report a vulnerability in the relay to security@perfloop.ai. That is the
address Perfloop publishes for security questions and reviews; it reaches the
people who maintain this repository. Include the commit or image digest you
looked at, how to reproduce, and what you believe the impact is. Do not open a
public issue for a vulnerability.

Supported: the `main` branch and the latest published image (`docs/image.md`).
A fix lands on `main` and is published as a new image; you choose when to roll
it, as with every relay change. This document makes no commitment beyond the
address: no response time, embargo, or reward program is published here.
